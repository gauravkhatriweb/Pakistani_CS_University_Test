## Detailed Information

### 1. National University of Sciences and Technology (NUST)

**Entry Test Name:** NUST Entry Test (NET)

**Test Dates 2025-2026:**
- **NET Series-1:** November 22 - December 10, 2025 (Registration: October 5 - November 25, 2025)
- **NET Series-2:** January - March 2026 (Registration: December 2025 - January 2026)
- **NET Series-3:** April 2026 (Registration: February - April 2026)
- **NET Series-4:** June - July 2026 (Registration: April - June 2026)

**Application Deadlines:**
- Series-1: October 5 - November 25, 2025
- Series-2: December 2025 - January 2026
- Series-3: February - April 2026
- Series-4: April - June 2026

**Additional Information:**
- Computer-Based NET (CBNET) at Islamabad and Quetta
- Paper-Based NET (PBNET) at Karachi and Gilgit
- SAT/ACT scores also accepted (Deadline: July 25, 2026)
- Official Source: [nust.edu.pk](https://nust.edu.pk)

## Important Notes

1. **Test Formats:** Most universities conduct computer-based tests (CBT) or paper-based tests (PBT)
2. **Alternative Tests:** Many universities accept SAT, ACT, or NTS scores as alternatives to their own tests
3. **Registration:** All applications are typically submitted online through official university portals
4. **Deadlines:** Deadlines are strict; late applications are usually not accepted
5. **Merit Calculation:** Each university has its own merit formula combining academic scores and test results
